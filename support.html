Freeroom Plus prices

The planning ledger is free. Freeroom Plus adds Insights, unlimited paycheck split rules, and unlimited savings goals. United States list prices:

  • Freeroom Plus Monthly. $4.99 USD per month. Length: 1 month. Auto-renewable. 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Freeroom Plus Yearly. $29.99 USD per year, billed once a year. Length: 1 year. Auto-renewable. 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Freeroom Plus Lifetime. $39.99 USD once. One-time purchase. It does not renew and has no free trial.

The price you pay is the price on the App Store purchase screen, which can differ by country. The same facts, including the auto-renewal sentences Apple requires, are in the Terms of Use.

Cancel, trial, and refunds

Payment will be charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. A subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the price of the plan you selected.

A 7-day free trial, when Apple offers it to a new subscriber, converts to the paid monthly or yearly plan unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Any unused portion of a free trial period, if offered, will be forfeited when the user purchases a subscription to that publication, where applicable.

To cancel auto-renew:

  1. On iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Or in Freeroom open Settings → Freeroom Plus → Manage Subscription.
  2. Select the Freeroom Plus plan.
  3. Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the trial or the billing period ends.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Buying Lifetime does not cancel a monthly or yearly plan you already have. Turn that plan off in Subscriptions if you do not want it to renew. Lifetime itself has nothing to cancel.

You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renewal in your Account Settings after purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Plus is locked

Open Settings → Freeroom Plus → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe or to buy Lifetime. Restore Purchases restores the Plus entitlement. It does not copy your planning records onto a new iPhone. Those stay on the phone where you entered them, unless you move a CSV or restore a device backup through Apple.

Banks and safe to spend

Does Freeroom connect to my bank?

No. Freeroom is a planning ledger. You record balances, paychecks, and transfers, and it shows what's protected and what's safe to spend. It does not move money, and it does not ask for a bank login.

What is "Safe to spend"?

Cash in your spending accounts, minus each account's safety buffer, unpaid bills assigned to it, and pending purchases. Bill Vault and savings balances never count, and pending deposits don't count until they post.

Bill Vault

The Bill Vault is a separate account (often a second checking account at your bank) that holds bill money. The Payday Assistant shows how much it needs before your next paycheck. The money stays at your bank. Freeroom records the plan and does not move the money.

Balance doesn't match

Open the account and tap Reconcile with Bank Balance. Freeroom records the difference as an adjustment, so the history stays explainable. The app does not sign in to the bank to fetch the balance. You enter the figure.

When a new month starts

A new month starts on the 1st. The first time you open Freeroom in a new month, last month is saved to history and any rollover is applied once.

Export or delete data

Settings → Prepare CSV Export saves a copy when you ask for one. You choose where it goes. Freeroom does not upload it.

Settings → Reset All Data erases Freeroom's records on that iPhone and returns the app to setup. Deleting the app also removes those on-device records. Neither step deletes a CSV you already saved, an iPhone backup stored by Apple, or an App Store subscription. Cancel a subscription separately, in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Is this financial advice?

No. Freeroom calculates a plan from numbers you enter. It is not a bank, and it does not provide financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. See the Terms of Use.
