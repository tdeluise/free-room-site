Does Freeroom connect to my bank?

No. Freeroom is a planning ledger. You record balances, paychecks and transfers, and it shows what's protected and what's safe to spend. It never moves money.

What is "Safe to spend"?

Cash in your spending accounts, minus each account's safety buffer, unpaid bills assigned to it, and pending purchases. Bill Vault and savings balances never count, and pending deposits don't count until they post.

What is the Bill Vault?

A separate account (often a second checking account at your bank) that holds bill money. The Payday Assistant shows how much it needs before your next paycheck.

My balance doesn't match my bank.

Open the account and tap Reconcile with Bank Balance. Freeroom records the difference as an adjustment, so your history stays explainable.

When does a new month start?

Automatically on the 1st. The first time you open Freeroom in a new month, last month is saved to history and any rollover is applied once.

What does Freeroom Plus cost, and how do I cancel?

Freeroom Plus is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year (prices vary by country), with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You can also buy Lifetime once for $39.99, with nothing to cancel. It adds Insights, unlimited paycheck split rules and unlimited savings goals; everything else is free. Cancel anytime in Freeroom under Settings → Freeroom Plus → Manage Subscription, or in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but Plus is locked.

Open Settings → Freeroom Plus → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I back up or delete my data?

Settings → Prepare CSV Export saves a copy. Settings → Reset All Data erases everything and returns to setup.
