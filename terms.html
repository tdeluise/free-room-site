The service

Freeroom is a paycheck-planning ledger for iPhone, made by Todd De Luise, an individual in Texas, USA.

You enter your own numbers. Freeroom shows a plan from those numbers, including a safe-to-spend figure, a Bill Vault for bill money, and savings. The planning records stay on your iPhone. There is no Freeroom account.

Freeroom does not hold funds, open a bank account, connect to a bank, change payroll, or move money. It is not a bank, lender, or broker. Safe to spend and the other figures are calculations from what you entered. They are not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Check them against your own records before you rely on them.

What Freeroom Plus includes

The planning ledger is free to use. Freeroom Plus is optional. While a monthly or yearly subscription is active, or after a Lifetime purchase on the same Apple Account, Plus adds:

  • Insights
  • Unlimited paycheck split rules
  • Unlimited savings goals

If a monthly or yearly subscription expires or is refunded, Plus can lock again. The free planning ledger stays available. Restore Purchases brings back a Plus entitlement tied to the same Apple Account. It does not move planning records from one iPhone to another. Those records stay on the iPhone where you entered them, unless you move a CSV yourself or restore an iPhone backup through Apple.

Free trial

New subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial of Freeroom Plus Monthly or Freeroom Plus Yearly. Apple decides whether an Apple Account is eligible. If a trial is offered, the purchase screen shows how long the trial lasts and the price that will be billed when it ends.

During the trial you can use Freeroom Plus. You are not charged for those 7 days. If you do not turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the trial converts to the paid plan you selected and payment is charged to your Apple Account: $4.99 USD for the first month, or $29.99 USD for the first year, or the price shown for your storefront.

Any unused portion of a free trial period, if offered, will be forfeited when the user purchases a subscription to that publication, where applicable.

Freeroom Plus Lifetime does not include a free trial.

Deleting Freeroom does not cancel a subscription. Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends if you want to avoid the next charge.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by Freeroom. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How to cancel or restore

To stop a monthly or yearly plan from renewing:

  1. Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on the iPhone, or Settings → Freeroom Plus → Manage Subscription in the app.
  2. Select the Freeroom Plus plan.
  3. Turn off auto-renew. Do this at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

You keep Plus through the end of the period you already started, unless Apple refunds it. The free ledger remains.

If Plus stays locked after a purchase, open Settings → Freeroom Plus → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. More help is on the support page.
