Privacy Policy

Freeroom does not collect the planning records you enter. They stay on your iPhone.

Effective November 15, 2026. This version replaces the policy dated November 8, 2026.

Short version. Freeroom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the numbers you enter. There is no Freeroom account and no bank login. Optional purchases are paid through your Apple Account, and Apple handles the payment. You can erase the on-device ledger with Settings → Reset All Data, or by deleting the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Freeroom app for iPhone and this website. Freeroom is made by Todd De Luise, an individual in Texas, USA. He is the person responsible for the app and for this policy.

Questions can go to todd.deluise1999@icloud.com or the support page.

This website

This site is static HTML and CSS. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, or advertising. It does not collect your name or email address unless you choose to send an email.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as an IP address in its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not receive those logs and does not use them.

What is not collected

Freeroom does not collect personal information. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or bank credentials. It does not connect to your bank or to any other financial institution.

Freeroom does not sell personal information. It does not share personal information for advertising, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores, on your iPhone, the planning records you type, so it can show your plan. Those records include the ledger accounts you create (for example a spending account or a Bill Vault), balances, transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals, paycheck split rules, and settings. A ledger account is a category you keep inside Freeroom. It is not a login.

Freeroom uses those records on the device to calculate and display your plan, including what is safe to spend, bill money, and savings. That use happens on the iPhone. The records are not uploaded to Todd De Luise and are not sent to a Freeroom server. There is no Freeroom server.

Backups

Freeroom does not sync your planning records through iCloud or any other cloud service. If you turn on backup for the iPhone (iCloud Backup, or a backup to a computer), Apple's backup can include the app's local data along with the rest of the device. That backup is controlled by you and by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Freeroom cannot see it. Restoring the iPhone from that backup can bring the local records back. That is Apple's device backup, not a Freeroom sync feature.

Purchases and Apple

Freeroom Plus is optional: an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices, renewal, the trial, and cancellation are described in the Terms of Use.

If you subscribe or buy Lifetime, Apple processes the payment through the App Store and StoreKit. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Freeroom does not receive your card number, Apple Account password, or billing address.

The app receives Apple's on-device confirmation of whether Freeroom Plus is active, so it can unlock Plus features. The developer does not run a server that receives your planning records or your purchase receipts. Sales reports Apple provides to a developer do not include the planning records you type into Freeroom.

Analytics, crashes, and tracking

Freeroom does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. It does not send usage data or crash reports to Todd De Luise or to any third-party analytics or crash-reporting company. It does not use the advertising identifier to track you.

The planning ledger works offline. The network use the app depends on is Apple's, for the App Store and for an optional Freeroom Plus purchase.

If you turn on Apple's own diagnostics, such as Share iPhone Analytics, in iPhone Settings, Apple may receive diagnostic information under Apple's privacy policy. That setting is between you and Apple. It is not a Freeroom reporting tool.

Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, Freeroom schedules them locally on your iPhone. Reminder details are not sent to the developer. You can turn reminders off in the app, and you can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

CSV export

A CSV file is created only when you ask for one, with Settings → Prepare CSV Export. You choose where to save or share that file. The developer does not receive the file unless you attach it to a message you send yourself.

Children

Freeroom is a general paycheck-planning app. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else. The app does not ask for a birth date or a parent's contact details.

If a child types records into Freeroom, those records stay on that iPhone. A parent can remove them with Settings → Reset All Data or by deleting the app. A parent with a question can email todd.deluise1999@icloud.com.

Third parties

Freeroom does not share user data with analytics tools, advertising networks, or other third-party SDKs. It does not provide user data to a parent company, subsidiary, or other related entity. There is no separate Freeroom service that receives it.

Two companies have a limited role that is theirs, not Freeroom's:

  • Apple distributes the app, processes an optional purchase, confirms on the device whether Plus is active, and, if you turn backup or Apple diagnostics on, handles those under Apple's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website under GitHub's privacy statement.

Their policies describe how they protect the data they process. Freeroom does not send them your planning records.

Retention, deletion, and consent

The developer does not keep a copy of your planning records, because they never leave the iPhone. There is no account to close and no server retention period.

To delete the on-device records, use Settings → Reset All Data. That erases Freeroom's data on that iPhone and returns the app to setup. Deleting the app also removes those on-device records.

Reset All Data does not delete a CSV you already exported. It does not delete an iPhone backup stored by Apple. Delete those copies where you saved them if you want them gone.

Canceling Freeroom Plus and deleting on-device data are separate steps. Cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings. Erase the ledger in the app, or delete the app, if you also want the records gone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The steps are on the Terms of Use page and the support page.

Because Freeroom does not collect personal information, the developer has no profile to show you, correct, or delete, and no stored consent to withdraw. You can stop using the app at any time, turn notifications off, cancel a purchase through Apple, and delete the on-device data as described above. If you email a privacy request, the developer can explain these practices. He cannot retrieve or delete planning records from a server, because he does not have them.

Security

Planning records sit in the app's local storage on your iPhone, protected by your device lock. Freeroom does not add a separate account password, because there is no account. If you export a CSV, protect that file the way you would any other note about your money.

Changes
