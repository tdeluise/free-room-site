Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Short version: Freeroom doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Freeroom stores

The accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals and settings you enter are saved on your device using Apple's on-device storage. If your device backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, that data is included in your device backup under Apple's terms.

What Freeroom doesn't do

  • No sign-up, account or login.
  • No connection to banks or financial institutions, and no bank credentials requested.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests of its own: the app works fully offline. The only exception is the optional Freeroom Plus subscription, which is handled entirely by Apple's App Store.
  • It doesn't hold funds, create bank accounts, change payroll or move money.

Subscriptions

Freeroom Plus is optional: a monthly or yearly subscription with a free trial for new subscribers, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple. Freeroom never receives your payment details or Apple Account information; it only receives Apple's confirmation of whether your subscription is active. You can cancel anytime in your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, they're scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data leaves your device.

Exports

CSV exports are created only when you ask, and you choose where to share them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset All Data in the app, or delete the app. Both remove all Freeroom data from the device.

Children

Freeroom isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
