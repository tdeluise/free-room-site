Freeroom

Know exactly what's safe to spend the moment your paycheck lands.

Freeroom turns every paycheck into a clear plan: record what landed, protect bill money in a Bill Vault, set aside savings, and see the cash that's truly free.

It is a planning ledger. The numbers stay on your iPhone. Freeroom does not connect to your bank, hold funds, or move money, and the figures it shows are not financial, tax, or investment advice.

Private by design. No account, no bank login, and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. The privacy policy describes that in full.

What you can do

Safe to spend

Cash in your spending accounts, minus each account's safety buffer, unpaid bills assigned to it, and pending purchases. Bill Vault and savings balances never count. Pending deposits don't count until they post.

Bill Vault

A separate place for bill money. The Payday Assistant shows how much it needs before your next paycheck.

Savings

Set money aside for savings goals. Unlimited savings goals are part of Freeroom Plus.

Your own records

Reconcile with Bank Balance when a number needs an adjustment. Export a CSV when you want a copy. Use Reset All Data in Settings when you want the ledger erased from the iPhone.

Billing, renewal, and cancellation

During each paid month or year of Freeroom Plus, you get Insights, unlimited paycheck split rules, and unlimited savings goals, along with the free planning ledger. A new subscriber who is eligible can start with a 7-day free trial of the monthly or yearly plan. When the trial ends, it converts to that paid plan and the Apple Account is charged unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Apple decides whether an Apple Account is eligible for the trial. The purchase screen shows the trial length and the price that will be billed when the trial ends.

  • Payment will be charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. Freeroom Plus Monthly renews at $4.99 USD per month. Freeroom Plus Yearly renews at $29.99 USD per year. Outside the United States, renewal is at the price displayed for that storefront.
  • You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renewal by going to your Account Settings after purchase. On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. In Freeroom: Settings → Freeroom Plus → Manage Subscription.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial period, if offered, will be forfeited when the user purchases a subscription to that publication, where applicable.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Freeroom Plus Lifetime is not a subscription. Payment for it is charged once to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Buying Lifetime does not cancel a monthly or yearly subscription. Turn that subscription off in Account Settings if you do not want it to renew.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
